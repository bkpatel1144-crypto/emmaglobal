The markets within our regional reach collectively represent more than USD 60 trillion in economic activity and approximately 4.9 billion people. They include international headquarters, advanced industrial economies, major manufacturing centres and rapidly developing business ecosystems. Our regional structure allows us to combine global standards with an understanding of local business environments, workforce practices, industry requirements and regulatory conditions.

Singapore is one of Asia’s leading centres for regional headquarters, professional services, investment and cross-border business management. China, Japan and South Korea represent some of the world’s largest automotive, electronics, engineering, chemicals and advanced-manufacturing markets.

Indonesia, Vietnam and Thailand continue to attract investment across manufacturing, automotive, electronics, logistics, consumer products and digital services. ASEAN attracted approximately USD 244 billion in foreign direct investment during 2025, demonstrating its increasing importance to international businesses.

Australia extends the region into a mature, highly regulated market with established strengths in mining and resources, agribusiness, construction and infrastructure, financial and professional services, education and hospitality. Its two-hour offset from Singapore allows a full shared working day across the region.

Industries and Organisations We Serve

Sector expertise applied consistently across all four regions.

Corporate Offices, Regional HQs, GCCs and Shared Services

We support regional headquarters, Global Capability Centres and shared-service organisations with cross-border HR operations, administrative processes, organisational governance, responsible AI adoption and corporate support.

Automotive and Auto Components

Automotive organisations operate through complex supply chains and specialised workforces. EMMA Global provides ESG, HR, AI, administration and governance support to manufacturers, component suppliers and engineering businesses.

Manufacturing and Heavy Engineering

We help industrial organisations strengthen workforce systems, ESG performance, operational governance, administrative efficiency and the responsible use of AI across their operations.

Construction, Infrastructure and Engineering

We support businesses managing project-based workforces, contractors, safety obligations, regulatory requirements, ESG commitments and multi-location administration.

Chemicals, Pharmaceuticals and APIs

We assist regulated and technically complex organisations with workforce governance, ESG implementation, organisational capability, administrative controls and corporate-compliance coordination.

Textiles, Apparel and Fashion Accessories

Textile and apparel supply chains face increasing expectations concerning labour standards, workplace safety, traceability and environmental performance. We help organisations improve ESG, HR, administration and supplier-management systems.

Transportation, Logistics and Warehousing

We support businesses managing distributed workforces, international supply chains, warehouses and transportation networks through HR transformation, ESG programmes, administration and AI-enabled process improvement.

Hotels, Restaurants and Hospitality

Our solutions address workforce engagement, employee turnover, seasonal recruitment, service quality, multi-location administration, ESG requirements and business-process automation.

Media, Entertainment and Creative Industries

We support production companies, media organisations, entertainment businesses, creative agencies and digital-content enterprises with HR, talent management, contracts administration, responsible AI and corporate support.

Information Technology and AI-Enabled Businesses

EMMA Global helps technology companies scale their workforce, establish responsible AI governance, implement HR and administrative systems, strengthen ESG practices and manage corporate growth.

Metals, Mining and Material Processing

These high-impact industries require strong environmental, workforce, safety and governance frameworks. We support ESG implementation, workforce systems, administration and organisational compliance.

Aerospace and Defence Manufacturing

We assist technically specialised and highly regulated organisations with workforce strategy, talent development, ESG, administrative controls, governance and responsible technology adoption.

Start-ups and Emerging Enterprises

Growing businesses can access integrated HR, ESG, AI, administration and corporate support without immediately establishing several separate internal departments.
